Frequently Asked Questions

What is Community Connect SA?

Community Connect SA connects high school learners with community service opportunities.

Who can use the website?

High school learners, parents, schools and community organisations can use the platform.

How do I find a volunteer opportunity?

Visit the Volunteer Opportunities page and browse the available categories.

How do I become a volunteer?

Select an opportunity and submit an enquiry through the enquiry form.

Can organisations list opportunities?

Organisations can contact Community Connect SA to enquire about becoming a participating organisation.
