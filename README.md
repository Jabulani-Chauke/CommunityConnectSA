## Project Overview

**Community Connect SA** is a website designed to help high school learners find meaningful and verified community service opportunities in South Africa.

The website connects learners with organisations and community projects where they can volunteer and contribute to their communities. It provides information about available volunteer opportunities, explains the purpose and mission of Community Connect SA, and allows users to make enquiries and access contact information.

The website was developed using **HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript**, with a focus on clear structure, simple navigation, responsive design and a user-friendly experience.

---

## Website Goals and Objectives

### Main Goal

The main goal of the Community Connect SA website is to make it easier for high school learners to discover, understand and enquire about community service opportunities.

### Objectives

The website aims to:

* Provide learners with information about community service opportunities.
* Make volunteer opportunities easy to find and understand.
* Provide information about Community Connect SA and its mission.
* Allow learners and organisations to submit enquiries.
* Provide clear contact information.
* Provide useful resources for learners interested in volunteering.
* Create a responsive website that works on both desktop and mobile devices.
* Provide simple and consistent navigation across all website pages.

## Key Features

### 1. Home Page

The Home page provides an introduction to Community Connect SA and includes:

* Hero section with a call-to-action.
* Introduction to the organisation.
* Featured community service opportunities.
* Links to find volunteer opportunities.
* Links to make enquiries.

### 2. About Us

The About Us page provides information about Community Connect SA, including:

* Organisation background.
* Mission and vision.
* Information about the organisation's purpose.
* Meet Our Team section.

### 3. Volunteer Opportunities

The Volunteer Opportunities page allows learners to explore different types of community service, including:

* Animal shelters.
* Environmental projects.
* Hospitals.
* Schools.
* Libraries.
* Old-age homes.

The page also includes:

* Search functionality for opportunities.
* Information about individual opportunities.
* Enquiry buttons for volunteer opportunities.

### 4. Enquiry Page

The Enquiry page allows users to submit questions or requests through an online form.

The form includes:

* Full name.
* Email address.
* Phone number.
* Enquiry type.
* Message.
* Submit button.

JavaScript is used to provide form interaction and functionality.

### 5. Contact Page

The Contact page provides ways for users to contact Community Connect SA.

It includes:

* Contact information.
* Email details.
* Telephone details.
* Office locations.
* Map sections.
* A link to the enquiry form.

### 6. FAQ Page

The FAQ page provides answers to common questions about Community Connect SA and volunteering.

Expandable questions and answers are implemented using HTML5:

`<details>` and `<summary>` elements.

### 7. Volunteer Resources

The Volunteer Resources page provides useful information to help learners prepare for community service.

Resources include:

* Volunteer checklist.
* Community service tips.
* Preparation information.
* Guidance for learners interested in volunteering.

### 8. Responsive Design

The website is designed to provide a consistent experience across different screen sizes.

Responsive features include:

* Mobile-friendly layouts.
* Responsive navigation.
* Flexible content sections.
* CSS media queries for smaller screens.
* Responsive images and page elements.
